Backups are only useful if recovery works

A backup strategy should cover which information matters, where copies are stored, how recovery is initiated and how restore results are verified.

Areas of assistance

  • Business data backup planning
  • Cloud backup solution discussions
  • Backup monitoring where included in an agreed service
  • Recovery planning and restoration support
  • Business continuity planning
  • Acronis-based solutions where available and appropriately licensed

Why a recovery plan matters

Backups alone do not confirm that data can be restored. Recovery planning defines priorities, restoration steps and the people responsible for action.

Frequently asked questions

Does ReliaServe guarantee that every restore will succeed?
No. Restore success depends on the systems, configuration and backup state. The goal is to monitor and test an agreed solution rather than make unsupported guarantees.

Is Acronis a ReliaServe partnership?
No partnership or certification status is implied. Acronis is a product area and access to services depends on eligibility and applicable terms.
