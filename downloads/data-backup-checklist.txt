Business Data Backup Checklist
Updated: 26 November 2026

SCOPE
[ ] Identify critical files, databases and configurations
[ ] Assign data owners
[ ] Document systems and applications needed for recovery

COPIES
[ ] At least three copies exist
[ ] At least two storage types are used
[ ] One copy is off-site or isolated
[ ] Access to backup infrastructure is restricted

RETENTION
[ ] Recovery points meet the business recovery point objective
[ ] Retention periods are documented

MONITORING
[ ] Backup successes and failures are reviewed
[ ] Expiry, capacity and credential alerts are assigned

RECOVERY
[ ] Restoration steps are documented
[ ] Representative data restores are tested
[ ] Application consistency is verified
[ ] Recovery results and dates are recorded
