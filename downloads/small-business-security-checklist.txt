Small Business Cybersecurity Checklist
Updated: 26 November 2026

ACCOUNT SECURITY
[ ] Multi-factor authentication enabled for critical accounts
[ ] Shared accounts identified and removed where possible
[ ] Former staff access removed promptly
[ ] Administrator access limited

DEVICE SECURITY
[ ] Operating systems and applications receive supported updates
[ ] Endpoint protection is active and reported
[ ] Lost or stolen devices can be remotely locked or wiped

EMAIL SECURITY
[ ] SPF, DKIM and DMARC considered for company domains
[ ] Staff know how to report suspicious messages
[ ] External sender warnings are enabled where useful

DATA AND RECOVERY
[ ] Critical data has a documented backup scope
[ ] A separate or isolated copy exists
[ ] Restore tests have been completed and documented
[ ] A continuity plan identifies recovery priorities

MONITORING
[ ] Unusual account and system events are reviewed
[ ] Log retention and alert ownership are documented
